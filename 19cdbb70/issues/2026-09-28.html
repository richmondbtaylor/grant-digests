Veteran Services Grant Deadlines

Week of September 28, 2026

This week's digest highlights opportunities supporting veteran health research and justice initiatives. The Defense Health Agency is accepting applications for three Military Burn research awards—Discovery, Patient-Centered Research, and Technology/Therapeutic Development—all closing October 21. These support organizations advancing treatment approaches for service members and veterans affected by burn injuries.

The Bureau of Justice Assistance is also accepting applications for veteran-focused justice programs. The FY 2026 Veterans Treatment Court Program closes October 29, while the National Center for Veterans Justice closes October 30. Both support organizations working to address justice system needs specific to veteran populations.
