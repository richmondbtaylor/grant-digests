Veteran Services Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest covers federal opportunities closing between now and late November 2026, spanning housing stability, education research, veteran justice programs, and military medical innovation. Whether you're supporting veterans experiencing homelessness, advancing special education research, or addressing burn care for service members, these deadlines represent direct pathways to federal funding.

The opportunities listed below are organized by closing date to help you prioritize applications. Each entry includes eligibility basics, award details, and direct links to official notices of funding opportunity (NOFOs). Review requirements carefully—application processes, match funding, and organizational qualifications vary by program.

---

**September 30, 2026**

**FY 2026 Continuum of Care Competition and Youth Homelessness Demonstration Program Grants** - **Agency:** Department of Housing and Urban Development - **Eligibility:** Nonprofit organizations, state/local governments, and public housing authorities addressing homelessness - **Award Amount:** Varies by project type and geography - **Link:** [HUD Continuum of Care NOFO](https://www.hud.gov/program_offices/special_needs_assistance_programs/coc)

---

**October 1, 2026**

**Research Training Programs in The Education Sciences (ALN 84.305B)** - **Agency:** Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Research - **Eligibility:** Institutions of higher education offering doctoral or postdoctoral training - **Award Amount:** Varies - **Link:** [IES NCER Training Programs](https://ies.ed.gov/ncer/)

**Statistical and Research Methodology in Education (ALN 84.305D)** - **Agency:** Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Education Research - **Eligibility:** Institutions of higher education and nonprofit research organizations - **Award Amount:** Varies - **Link:** [IES NCER Methodology Programs](https://ies.ed.gov/ncer/)

**Research Training Programs in Special Education (ALN 84.324B)** - **Agency:** Institute of Education Sciences, National Center for Special Education Research - **Eligibility:** Institutions of higher education offering doctoral or postdoctoral training in special education - **Award Amount:** Varies - **Link:** [IES NCSER Training Programs](https://ies.ed.gov/ncser/)

---

**October 21, 2026**

**DoW Military Burn, Discovery Award** - **Agency:** Defense Health Agency Contracting Activity - **Eligibility:** Researchers and institutions investigating military burn injury prevention, treatment, or recovery - **Award Amount:** Varies - **Link:** [Defense Health Agency Burn Research](https://cdmrp.army.mil/burn)

**DoW Military Burn, Patient-Centered Research Award** - **Agency:** Defense Health Agency Contracting Activity - **Eligibility:** Researchers and institutions with veteran or service member input in burn research design - **Award Amount:** Varies - **Link:** [Defense Health Agency Burn Research](https://cdmrp.army.mil/burn)
