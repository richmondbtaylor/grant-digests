Week of September 29, 2026

This week's funding digest highlights opportunities across justice, transportation, and community resilience sectors. While most deadlines cluster in early October, organizations should begin preliminary eligibility reviews now to meet submission windows. The Bureau of Justice Assistance leads this cycle with two separate programs—the Crisis Response Training Program (October 5) and the Edward Byrne Memorial Justice Assistance Grant for local jurisdictions (October 9)—both supporting law enforcement and public safety infrastructure.

Beyond criminal justice, federal agencies are accepting applications for specialized training, infrastructure resilience, and community development initiatives. The DOT's PROTECT program (October 9) funds transportation safety projects, while the Forest Service's Urban and Community Forestry Challenge (October 12) supports local environmental work. Organizations should verify specific eligibility requirements and match organizational capacity to program scope before applying.
