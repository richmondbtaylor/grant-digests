Rural Grant Deadlines

Week of September 28, 2026

This week's federal opportunities span housing stability, water infrastructure, health workforce development, and community safety—areas where rural organizations consistently face funding gaps. The most immediate deadlines cluster around late September and early October, with housing and water programs closing September 30 and energy infrastructure following October 9. Rural health groups should prioritize opportunities from HUD, HRSA, and the Indian Health Service, while water and utility systems can access Rural Utilities Service and EPA programs. Several opportunities target tribal communities and historically underserved institutions specifically.

The digest below reflects open competitions as of this week. Application requirements, eligibility details, and submission procedures vary significantly by program; organizations should review each NOFO directly before committing staff time. Deadlines are firm federal dates. This summary does not predict funding likelihood or outcomes for any applicant.
