Rural Health Funding Weekly

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest covers two federal opportunities relevant to rural health operations. The first addresses workforce development in a specialized infrastructure sector. The second represents a major HRSA competition cycle that affects rural health service delivery and staffing.

Both opportunities close within the same week in October 2026. Organizations should review eligibility requirements and submission procedures now, as both require substantive applications. Compliance review is necessary before submission.

---

**Fiscal Year (FY) 2027 Service Area Competition (SAC)**

Health Resources and Services Administration

Closes: October 19, 2026

The Service Area Competition is HRSA's primary mechanism for awarding grants to health centers serving rural and underserved populations. This cycle funds primary care access expansion, workforce recruitment and retention, and operational infrastructure. Eligible applicants include federally qualified health centers (FQHCs), rural health clinics, and health center program grantees. Detailed eligibility criteria and application requirements are available through HRSA's grants portal. Organizations should verify current funding priorities and match requirements before developing proposals.

**Innovative Water Infrastructure Workforce Development Grant**

Environmental Protection Agency

Closes: October 15, 2026

This EPA program supports workforce training and development in water infrastructure sectors. Rural health facilities with water system operations or environmental health components may find relevance depending on project scope. Applicants should confirm alignment with EPA's stated program objectives and rural health eligibility before investing application resources.
