Rural Health Funding Weekly

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest covers two federal opportunities relevant to rural health operations. The EPA's Innovative Water Infrastructure Workforce Development Grant closes October 15, 2026, and supports training programs for water utility workers. HRSA's FY 2027 Service Area Competition closes October 19, 2026, and addresses primary care access through competitive allocation of health center resources.

One opportunity targets workforce development in infrastructure; the other focuses on primary care service expansion. Both deadlines fall within the same week. Organizations should review eligibility requirements and application procedures on each agency's grants portal before submitting.

**Innovative Water Infrastructure Workforce Development Grant** Environmental Protection Agency | Closes October 15, 2026

Supports development and delivery of workforce training programs for water utility operators and technicians. Eligible applicants include nonprofits, state and local governments, and educational institutions. Review EPA's Water Infrastructure Finance and Innovation Act (WIFIA) program guidance for specific scope and budget parameters.

**FY 2027 Service Area Competition (SAC)** Health Resources and Services Administration | Closes October 19, 2026

Allocates funding for Federally Qualified Health Centers (FQHCs) and primary care services in underserved areas. Administered through HRSA's Bureau of Primary Health Care. Applicants must meet FQHC designation requirements or apply as eligible entities. Consult HRSA's GRANTS.GOV listing for application instructions and service area definitions.
