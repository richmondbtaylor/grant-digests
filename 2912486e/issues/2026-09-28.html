Fire & EMS Grant Deadlines

Week of September 28, 2026

This week's digest highlights four federal funding opportunities with deadlines spanning October through early November 2026. Agencies including the Agricultural Marketing Service, DOT Federal Highway Administration, United States Coast Guard, and National Institute of Food and Agriculture are accepting applications across preparedness, resilience, and hazard mitigation focus areas.

First responders and emergency management professionals should review eligibility requirements for each program early. The Cold Chain Grants for Emergency Food Assistance closes October 1, followed by the PROTECT Competitive Grant Program (October 9), State Recreational Boating Safety Grant Program (October 30), and Emergency Citrus Disease Research and Extension Pre-Applications (November 2).
