# Federal Grants Digest – Closing Within 60 Days

This week's digest covers three federal opportunities with approaching deadlines. Two programs close in October, and one in early November. None of these grants are administered by traditional fire or EMS funding agencies; however, emergency management and public safety organizations sometimes partner on multi-agency applications or coordinate with local government entities that manage these programs.

Review eligibility requirements carefully before committing staff time to applications. Match rates and allowable costs vary significantly by program. This digest provides structural information only; contact each agency directly to confirm current requirements and verify your organization's eligibility status.

---

## GRANT SUMMARIES

### 1. Cold Chain Grants for Emergency Food Assistance **Agency:** Agricultural Marketing Service **Deadline:** October 1, 2026 (58 days from publication) **Eligibility:** Contact AMS directly; typically available to state agencies, nonprofits, and food distribution networks **Match Rate:** Not specified in source data; confirm with agency **Key Action:** Request full NOFO (Notice of Funding Opportunity) from AMS to verify fire/EMS applicant eligibility

---

### 2. State Recreational Boating Safety (RBS) Grant Program **Agency:** United States Coast Guard **Deadline:** October 30, 2026 (87 days from publication) **Eligibility:** State boating safety agencies and designated state administrators; fire departments and EMS may be eligible as subrecipients under state applications **Match Rate:** Not specified in source data; confirm with USCG **Key Action:** Contact your state's boating safety office to determine if your agency can be named as a subrecipient

---

### 3. Emergency Citrus Disease Research and Extension Pre-Applications **Agency:** National Institute of Food and Agriculture **Deadline:** November 2, 2026 (95 days from publication) **Eligibility:** Universities, research institutions, and extension services; fire/EMS eligibility unlikely **Match Rate:** Not specified in source data **Key Action:** Review only if your organization partners with a university or extension service on emergency response research

---

## COMPLIANCE NOTE

All three programs fall outside traditional fire/EMS federal funding streams (FEMA, AFG, SAFER). Verify your organization's legal eligibility before investing application resources. Contact each agency's grants office for current NOFOs and match requirements.
