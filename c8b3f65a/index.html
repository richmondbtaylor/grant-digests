Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest includes opportunities across restoration ecology, land acquisition, and workforce development. Federal agencies are accepting applications for research on ecosystem indicators in Florida's Everglades and estuaries, including work with American alligators, crocodiles, wading birds, and sportfish communities. Water infrastructure and salinity monitoring projects are also open, alongside the Desalination and Water Purification Research Program. Land trusts and conservation groups can explore acquisition funding through the Cooperative Endangered Species Conservation Fund and Highlands Conservation Act programs, which support both base and competitive rounds.

Coastal and watershed programs remain a priority across multiple agencies. The Fish and Wildlife Service is accepting proposals for its Coastal Program and Partners for Fish and Wildlife initiatives, while the Bureau of Land Management is recruiting for its Youth Conservation Corps. Additional opportunities include the National Urban and Community Forestry Challenge Cost Share Grant, Community Forest and Open Space Conservation Program, and the Quagga and Zebra Mussel Action Plan implementation grants for western states. The American Battlefield Protection Program is also accepting land acquisition applications, and a new initiative focuses on outdoor and heritage tourism development in the Chesapeake Bay Watershed.
