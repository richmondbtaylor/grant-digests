Grant Match Weekly – Conservation Edition

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest highlights 26 federal environmental grants closing between late September and November 2026. Federal agencies across water, wildlife, forestry, and coastal restoration are actively funding projects—with particular concentration in Everglades and Florida Bay ecosystem research (Fort Worth District), Fish and Wildlife Service land acquisition and habitat conservation, and Forest Service community forestry initiatives. Early September is the moment to assess eligibility and begin assembling match documentation, since most deadlines fall within the next eight weeks.

The strongest opportunities for land trusts and small nonprofits appear in habitat acquisition, coastal program work, and restoration implementation. Fish and Wildlife Service programs dominate the calendar with five separate deadlines in October and November; Bureau of Reclamation and EPA rounds support water infrastructure and tribal resilience work. Review agency websites now to confirm match requirements, indirect cost caps, and whether your organization meets geographic or programmatic eligibility thresholds—many of these opportunities carry specific restrictions on applicant type or project scope.
