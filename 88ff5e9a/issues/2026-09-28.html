Community Health Grant Deadlines

Week of September 28, 2026

This week's digest highlights funding opportunities across research, global health security, and substance use services. The Department of Housing and Urban Development's Continuum of Care Competition closes September 30, 2026, supporting homelessness interventions and youth demonstration programs. Multiple National Institutes of Health initiatives are open through mid-October, including centers focused on substance use disorder pharmacotherapeutics, precision medicine with AI integration, and behavioral health research for specific populations.

Global health security represents a significant focus this cycle, with the Centers for Disease Control offering multiple partnership-based opportunities in Ghana, Ethiopia, Indonesia, Sierra Leone, Côte d'Ivoire, and Senegal through early October. Additional CDC programs address laboratory systems strengthening, travelers' health surveillance, and emerging infectious disease research. The Health Resources and Services Administration's FY 2027 Service Area Competition (October 19) and the Bureau of Justice Assistance's substance use training program (November 2) round out key deadlines for community-based organizations and clinical partners.
