Community Health Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest highlights 15 federal opportunities spanning health, infrastructure, environmental, and community development—with five closing within the next two weeks. FQHCs and rural clinics should prioritize the HRSA Service Area Competition (closes October 19) and CDC-supported global health security initiatives in Ethiopia and Senegal (both close October 2) if your organization has relevant capacity. Several NIH research mechanisms remain open through mid-October for centers with research infrastructure and clinical expertise.

The opportunities below reflect a mix of direct service funding, infrastructure investment, and research validation grants. While some programs target specific populations or geographies (Indian Health Service, rural utilities, tribal consortia), others support cross-sector initiatives in mental health, precision medicine, and infectious disease prevention. Review eligibility requirements carefully—several NIH mechanisms explicitly exclude or limit clinical trial components. Use the checklists and match scores to assess organizational readiness before committing staff time to applications.
