Community Health Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest highlights nine federal funding opportunities with closing dates between now and late November 2026. The mix spans research infrastructure, global health security, mental health services, workforce development, and maternal-child health—reflecting current federal priorities across NIH, CDC, HRSA, IHS, and ACF. Several opportunities align directly with FQHC and rural clinic operations, particularly the HRSA Service Area Competition and IHS outreach initiatives.

Review the eligibility filters and match scores below to identify which opportunities fit your organization's mission and capacity. Each entry includes a readiness checklist to guide your application timeline. Note that deadlines cluster in mid-to-late October; prioritize those submissions now if your organization meets core eligibility criteria.

## **OPPORTUNITIES CLOSING WITHIN 60 DAYS**

### **1. Logistics Center for Precision Medicine with AI: Integrating Imaging with Multimodal Data (PRIMED-AI) (U24)** **Agency:** National Institutes of Health **Closes:** October 2, 2026 ⚠️ **URGENT** **Award Range:** $500K–$2M+ (estimated, U24 mechanism)

**Eligibility Filters:** - Domestic institutions (academic medical centers, research hospitals, FQHCs with research infrastructure preferred) - Clinical trials **not allowed** - Multidisciplinary teams required (informatics, imaging, clinical)

**Match Score for FQHCs/Rural Clinics:** ⭐⭐ (Low–Medium) *Rationale:* Requires significant AI/imaging infrastructure; better fit for academic partners or large health systems. Rural clinics may partner as clinical data sites.

**Application Readiness Checklist:** - [ ] Confirm institutional research capacity and IRB infrastructure - [ ] Identify AI/imaging technology partners if not in-house - [ ] Draft letters of commitment from collaborating institutions - [ ] Outline data-sharing agreements and governance - [ ] Prepare preliminary data on imaging or multimodal datasets

### **2. Expanding Global Health Security Through Local Partnerships in Ethiopia** **Agency:** Centers for Disease Control and Prevention – Global Health Center **Closes:** October 2, 2026 ⚠️ **URGENT** **Award Range:** $300K–$800K (estimated)

**Eligibility Filters:** - U.S.-based organizations with established Ethiopia presence or partnerships - Focus on disease surveillance, workforce capacity, or laboratory strengthening - Preference for organizations with prior CDC/global health funding

**Match Score for FQHCs/Rural Clinics:** ⭐ (Low) *Rationale:* Geographically specific to Ethiopia; requires international partnerships and compliance infrastructure.

**Application Readiness Checklist:** - [ ] Confirm existing or planned partnerships in Ethiopia - [ ] Document USAID/CDC compliance certifications - [ ] Outline surveillance or capacity-building objectives aligned with Ethiopian MOH - [ ] Prepare budget narrative with in-country cost justification
