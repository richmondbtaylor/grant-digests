Arts Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week brings a concentrated window of opportunity for museums and libraries, with the Institute of Museum and Library Services opening its full 2027 grant portfolio on a single deadline. Whether you're a small museum seeking foundational support, a cultural institution focused on African American or Latino history, or a library serving Native communities, there's likely a fit in this cycle. The NEA and National Park Service also have programs closing sooner if heritage partnerships or outdoor tourism align with your work.

We've ranked these by deadline urgency so you can prioritize your calendar. Most opportunities close in mid-November, giving you time to gather materials and clarify your project scope—but "time" moves fast in grant work.
