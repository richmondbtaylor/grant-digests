Arts Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week brings a rare convergence of opportunity: the Institute of Museum and Library Services has opened eight museum-focused grants and five library programs, all closing November 13th. That's your biggest window for arts and cultural work right now. Meanwhile, the National Endowment for the Arts Partnership Agreement Grants close September 29th—less than two weeks away—making that your sprint deadline if you haven't started an application.

The good news is that most of these opportunities sit in the 6–8 week range, giving you genuine time to gather materials, talk to program officers, and build a thoughtful case. We've ranked everything below by deadline urgency and relevance to small arts nonprofits. Start with what closes soonest, but don't sleep on the mid-November deadlines—those IMLS grants move fast once you're ready.
