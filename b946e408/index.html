Arts Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week brings a rare convergence of deadlines across federal arts and cultural funding—with the NEA's Partnership Agreement Grants closing in just over a week, followed by a significant cluster of IMLS museum and library opportunities in mid-November. If you've been sitting on a partnership or collaborative project, the NEA window is your signal to move fast. For museum and library leaders, the November 13 deadline represents one of the year's broadest funding windows, with IMLS opening multiple streams tailored to different organizational sizes, missions, and communities.

We've ranked these opportunities by how urgently they need your attention and how closely they typically align with small nonprofit capacity. Whether you're a scrappy community museum, a library serving Native communities, or an arts organization building new partnerships, there's likely a fit here—but the clock is ticking on several fronts. Use this digest to identify which programs match your work, then block time this week to assess your readiness.
