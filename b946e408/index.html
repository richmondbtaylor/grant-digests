Arts Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week brings a concentrated window of opportunity, with the Institute of Museum and Library Services opening fifteen competitive grants across museums and libraries—all closing November 13th. Whether you run a small museum, serve Native communities, or lead workforce development in the cultural sector, IMLS has a program designed for your work. The NEA's Partnership Agreement Grants close much sooner (September 29th), so if arts partnerships are central to your mission, that deadline deserves immediate attention.

Below you'll find every opportunity closing within 60 days, organized by deadline. Each entry links directly to the funder's guidelines so you can assess fit quickly. Start with the earliest closes and work backward—that September 29th NEA deadline will arrive faster than the November cluster.
