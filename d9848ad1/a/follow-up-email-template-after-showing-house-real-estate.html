Follow Up Email Template After Showing House: A Practical Guide for Real Estate Agents

After you show a home to a buyer, the work is far from over. The follow-up email you send within the next 24 hours can be the difference between a buyer who moves forward and one who goes silent. This guide gives you real templates, examples, and a step-by-step process you can use today.

Why Follow Up Matters After a House Showing

When buyers leave a showing, they are in a decision-making mindset. They have questions, impressions, and feelings about the property. If you wait too long to reach out, another agent may contact them first, or they may lose momentum. A timely follow-up email keeps you top of mind and gives you a chance to address concerns, answer questions, and move the sale forward.

The Best Time to Send Your Follow Up Email

Send your follow-up email within 2 to 4 hours of the showing, or the same day at the latest. If the showing happens in the morning, send the email by early afternoon. If it happens in the afternoon, send it by evening or the next morning. The sooner you send it, the fresher the property will be in the buyer's mind.

What Your Follow Up Email Should Include

A strong follow-up email has five key parts:

  1. A warm greeting that uses the buyer's name
  2. A thank you for their time and interest
  3. A specific reference to something from the showing
  4. Answers to questions or next steps
  5. A clear call to action

Follow Up Email Template One: The Warm and Personal Approach

Use this template when you had a good rapport with the buyers and they seemed interested:

Subject Line: Thank You for Visiting 123 Oak Street Today

Email Body:

Hi [Buyer Name],

Thank you so much for taking the time to view 123 Oak Street this afternoon. I really enjoyed showing you the home and hearing your thoughts about the neighborhood.

I wanted to follow up on a few things we discussed. You mentioned that the kitchen was important to you, and I think you will appreciate the recent updates to the appliances and countertops. I am also attaching information about the local schools in the area, which you asked about.

If you have any questions about the property or would like to schedule another viewing, please let me know. I am here to help make this process as smooth as possible for you.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards, [Your Name] [Your Phone Number] [Your Email]

Follow Up Email Template Two: The Problem Solver

Use this template when the buyer raised concerns or objections during the showing:

Subject Line: Answers to Your Questions About 456 Maple Drive

Email Body:

Hi [Buyer Name],

Thank you for viewing 456 Maple Drive today. I know you had some questions about the roof and the HVAC system, and I wanted to get you some answers right away.

I contacted the seller's agent, and here is what I found out: The roof was replaced in 2018 and comes with a 10-year warranty. The HVAC system was serviced this year and is in good working order. I am attaching the inspection reports for your review.

These are common concerns, and I am glad you asked. Knowing the facts helps you make the best decision. Please let me know if you need any other information or if you would like to move forward with an offer.

I am available to discuss next steps at your convenience.

Best regards, [Your Name] [Your Phone Number] [Your Email]

Follow Up Email Template Three: The Gentle Nudge

Use this template when the buyer seemed lukewarm or uncertain:

Subject Line: Let's Talk About 789 Elm Court

Email Body:

Hi [Buyer Name],

I hope you are having a great week. I wanted to reach out and see if you had any additional thoughts about 789 Elm Court, which we viewed on [date].

Sometimes buyers need a little time to process a property. If you are still interested in learning more, I would be happy to arrange another showing or answer any questions you might have. If the property is not the right fit, I would also like to know what we should focus on in future showings.

Either way, I am here to help. Feel free to call or email me whenever you are ready to talk.

Best regards, [Your Name] [Your Phone Number] [Your Email]

Follow Up Email Checklist

Before you hit send, go through this checklist:

  • Did you use the buyer's correct name and spelling?
  • Did you send the email within 24 hours of the showing?
  • Did you reference something specific from the showing?
  • Did you answer at least one question or concern they raised?
  • Did you include your phone number and email address?
  • Did you include a clear next step or call to action?
  • Did you proofread for spelling and grammar?
  • Is your tone friendly and professional?

What to Do If They Don't Respond

If you do not hear back within 3 to 5 days, send a second follow-up email or make a phone call. Keep your tone light and helpful, not pushy. You might say, "I wanted to check in and see if you had any other questions about the property. I am here to help whenever you are ready."

Final Thoughts

A good follow-up email shows buyers that you care about their experience and are ready to help them move forward. Use the templates above as a starting point, and adjust them to match your style and the specific situation. The key is to be prompt, personal, and helpful.
