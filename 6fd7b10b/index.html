# This Week's Federal Funding Digest

Federal agencies are accepting applications across multiple sectors through mid-November, with numerous opportunities explicitly designated for tribal governments, tribal organizations, and tribal colleges and universities. This digest tracks programs closing within the next 60 days, covering areas including education, infrastructure, justice, environmental management, broadband connectivity, and cultural services. Many of these opportunities include tribal set-asides or are exclusively available to Native American entities.

Below you'll find a table of current opportunities organized by close date, along with sponsoring agencies. Review each program's full solicitation for specific eligibility requirements, matching fund obligations, and application complexity before submitting. An RSS feed is available for real-time updates on new postings and deadline changes.
