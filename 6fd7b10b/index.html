Tribal Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest covers federal funding opportunities closing between now and mid-November 2026, with a strong focus on tribal set-asides and direct tribal eligibility. The opportunities span education, infrastructure, environmental management, cultural institutions, and justice systems—reflecting the breadth of federal support available to tribal governments and tribal organizations. Many of these programs are specifically designed for tribal colleges, tribal consortia, and Native American entities, ensuring resources reach communities with direct tribal involvement.

As you review these opportunities, pay close attention to matching requirements, which vary significantly by program and funder. Application complexity ranges from straightforward grant applications to more technical proposals requiring detailed project plans or research components.
