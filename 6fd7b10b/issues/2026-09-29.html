Tribal Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest highlights seventeen federal opportunities closing within the next sixty days, spanning infrastructure, education, environmental management, justice, and cultural services. These programs represent direct funding pathways for tribal governments, tribal organizations, and Native American-serving institutions, with most carrying explicit tribal set-asides or Native American eligibility requirements. Application complexity ranges from streamlined basic grants to competitive research proposals requiring institutional capacity and matching funds.

The opportunities cluster around three primary closing windows: late September through mid-October for education and justice programs, and November for environmental, broadband, library, and drought resilience initiatives. Applicants should prioritize reviewing matching requirements, indirect cost policies, and technical assistance availability early in the application process, as these factors significantly affect net award value and implementation feasibility. Tribal organizations are encouraged to contact program officers for clarification on eligibility, allowable costs, and submission procedures before deadlines approach.
