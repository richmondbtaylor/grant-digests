Tribal Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest highlights seventeen federal funding opportunities designed specifically for tribal governments, tribal organizations, tribal colleges, and Native American entities—all closing within the next sixty days. The programs span critical areas including infrastructure, education, environmental management, public safety, broadband access, and cultural services. Many carry explicit tribal set-asides or are restricted to Native American applicants, ensuring resources reach communities with demonstrated need and unique governance structures.

Applicants should review each opportunity's matching requirements, which vary significantly across agencies and program types. Application complexity ranges from streamlined processes to detailed multi-component submissions requiring institutional capacity, technical expertise, or collaborative partnerships. Early engagement with program officers and careful attention to eligibility criteria, deadline dates, and submission formats will strengthen competitiveness and reduce processing delays.

**Opportunities Closing September 30 – October 21, 2026:**

- **Staff Support to Tribal Radioactive Materials Transportation Committee** (DOE Idaho Field Office, closes 9/30) – Technical engagement support for tribal participation in federal radioactive materials planning.

- **Indian Community Development Block Grant (ICDBG) Imminent Threat Program** (HUD, closes 9/30) – Community development funding for imminent threats; verify matching and application complexity with HUD.

- **Tribal Colleges Education Equity Grants Program** (NIFA, closes 10/7) – Education support restricted to tribal colleges; review cost-share requirements.

- **HBCU and TCU Equipment/Instrumentation Research and Education Program** (Army Materiel Command, closes 10/9) – Equipment funding for tribal colleges; applicant capacity and institutional partnerships typically required.

- **Tribal Colleges and Universities Program (TCUP)** (NSF, closes 10/14) – Competitive grants for tribal college research and education; moderate to high application complexity.

- **FY26 Coordinated Tribal Assistance Solicitation** (DOJ Bureau of Justice Assistance, closes 10/15) – Multi-program justice funding; coordinate applications across BJA solicitations.

- **BJA FY 2026 Tribal Justice Training and Technical Assistance Program** (BJA, closes 10/21) – Technical assistance and training for tribal justice systems.

**Opportunities Closing November 2 – November 20, 2026:**
