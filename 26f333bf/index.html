Small Business Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest spotlights federal opportunities spanning marine research, semiconductor manufacturing, small-business innovation, and infrastructure development. NOAA's three Broad Agency Announcements remain open through September 2026, offering sustained pathways for research and development partnerships. Simultaneously, the RESTORE Act Direct Component and CHIPS Incentives Program present significant construction and capital-equipment funding windows extending into November 2026.

Small manufacturers and technology firms should prioritize NSF's Small Business Innovation Research Phase I and Phase II programs, closing November 4th, which emphasize scientific instrumentation development. Infrastructure-focused organizations may explore Impact Aid Discretionary Construction Grants and the Tribal Broadband Connectivity Program, both closing mid-November. Each opportunity carries distinct eligibility and technical requirements; applicants should review full solicitations before submission to confirm alignment with their business profile and capabilities.
