K-12 School Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest highlights 16 federal opportunities spanning research, infrastructure, safety, and equity initiatives—with deadlines ranging from late September through mid-November 2026. Whether your district prioritizes STEM advancement, school safety, youth prevention, or institutional capacity-building, these programs offer pathways to strengthen educational outcomes and community partnerships. Early planning is essential; most opportunities require multi-section applications, institutional commitments, and compliance documentation.

The opportunities below reflect diverse funding agencies and focus areas. Some target specific populations (tribal colleges, HBCUs, rural communities), while others support broad research and education goals. Review eligibility requirements carefully, as many programs have geographic, institutional, or programmatic restrictions. Use the checklists provided to assess readiness and begin internal coordination with relevant departments—compliance, finance, research, and program leadership—well ahead of submission deadlines.
