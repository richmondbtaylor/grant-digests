K-12 School Grant Deadlines

Week of September 29, 2026

This week's digest highlights 17 federal opportunities spanning research, infrastructure, safety, and early childhood—with deadlines ranging from late September through November 2026. Whether your district serves tribal communities, historically underrepresented populations, or all students, these grants address priorities from STEM education and school violence prevention to public health systems and fair housing outreach. Many align with K-12 strategic goals around equity, teacher development, and student support.

We've organized these opportunities by deadline urgency and filtered key eligibility markers so you can quickly identify the best fit for your district's capacity and mission. Use the checklists below to assess readiness and begin preliminary research now—even grants closing in October benefit from early planning. Each entry links to the official funding announcement where you'll find detailed requirements, allowable activities, and submission portals.

**DEADLINE THIS WEEK & NEXT (Sept 30 – Oct 14)**

**Rural Decentralized Water System Grant Program** | Rural Utilities Service | Closes 2026-09-30 Rural districts serving populations under 10,000 may qualify. Funding supports water infrastructure planning and construction. Begin eligibility verification immediately with your state rural development office.

**IES NCER: Statistical and Research Methodology in Education (ALN 84.305D)** | Department of Education | Closes 2026-10-01 Districts partnering with university researchers on education methodology studies. Typically $150K–$600K awards. Confirm co-investigator commitments and institutional review board readiness now.

**EDU Core Research** | National Science Foundation | Closes 2026-10-01 STEM education research partnerships. Verify NSF FastLane account access and institutional research office support this week.

**Tribal Colleges Education Equity Grants Program** | National Institute of Food and Agriculture | Closes 2026-10-07 Tribal-serving institutions and districts. Agriculture and STEM focus. Confirm tribal eligibility documentation.

**Research and Education Program for HBCUs and TCUs: Equipment/Instrumentation** | Dept of the Army | Closes 2026-10-09 Historically Black Colleges, Universities, and Tribal Colleges. Lab and classroom equipment. Verify institutional classification and equipment specifications.

**Tribal Colleges and Universities Program (TCUP)** | National Science Foundation | Closes 2026-10-14 Tribal institutions. STEM capacity building. Confirm tribal college status and institutional research infrastructure.

**Research Experiences for Teachers (RET) in Engineering and Computer Science** | National Science Foundation | Closes 2026-10-14 Districts with teacher-researcher partnerships in engineering/CS. Summer stipends and curriculum development. Confirm teacher and university partner availability.
